LMSA is available on the Google Play Store for any Android device running Android 6.0 or higher. Download is free — you can start chatting immediately with the free tier, or upgrade once with a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads and unlock all features.

## Download from Google Play

Tap the badge below or search **"LMSA"** in the Google Play Store. The developer is **TechMitten LLC**.

[![Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lmsa.app)

Direct link: [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lmsa.app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lmsa.app)

## System requirements

| Requirement | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Android version | 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher |
| Internet connection | Required for OpenRouter and cloud providers |
| Local server mode | Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi network |

<Note>
LMSA does not run AI models on your device. For local server mode, the model runs on your PC — your phone only needs to reach it over your home Wi-Fi network. For cloud mode, you need an active internet connection and an API key from your chosen provider.
</Note>

## Free vs. premium

LMSA's core functionality is available for free. The free tier shows ads. A single one-time in-app purchase through Google Play removes all ads and unlocks every premium feature permanently — there are no subscriptions or recurring charges.

| Feature | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Connect to LM Studio | Yes | Yes |
| Connect to Ollama | Yes | Yes |
| Connect to OpenRouter | Yes | Yes |
| Connect to custom endpoints | Yes | Yes |
| Core chat, personas, TTS | Yes | Yes |
| File and image uploads | Yes | Yes |
| Web search (Brave Search) | Yes | Yes |
| Biometric lock | Yes | Yes |
| Ads | Yes | Removed |
| AI image generation | 2 images (lifetime) | Unlimited |
| Character Card AI generation | No | Yes |

<Tip>
  You can purchase premium at any time from the **Settings** screen. The purchase is tied to your Google account, so it carries over if you reinstall the app or switch devices.
</Tip>

## First-launch experience

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the terms">
    On first launch, LMSA displays its terms of service and privacy policy. Review and accept them to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the setup wizard">
    LMSA walks you through selecting your AI provider type (local server or cloud) and entering your connection details. You can skip this and configure it later in **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant permissions (optional)">
    LMSA may request microphone access for voice input and storage access for file attachments. These are optional — you can grant or deny them and adjust permissions later in Android system settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Once your provider is configured and a model is selected, you're ready to send your first message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Local server connections may be unencrypted. Use LMSA on trusted networks only — avoid connecting to local servers over public or shared Wi-Fi.
</Warning>
