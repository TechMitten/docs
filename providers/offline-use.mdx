Offline mode lets you chat with AI models running on your own computer without requiring an internet connection. This provides maximum privacy and full control over your data.

## What's available offline

When using offline mode with local servers, the following features are available:

- **Local LM Studio Models**: Run models loaded in your LM Studio server on your computer
- **Ollama Models**: Use models from your Ollama installation
- **Standard Chat**: Send messages and receive responses from your local model
- **File Attachments**: Attach files to chats for analysis
- **Text-to-Speech**: Synthesize AI responses using available voices
- **Templates & Character Cards**: Use system prompts and roleplay characters

## What's not available offline

The following features require an internet connection and are not available when using local servers:

- **OpenRouter Cloud Models**: Cannot access remote cloud AI services without internet
- **Web Search API**: Web search is not available in offline mode; the feature requires internet connectivity to query search engines
- **Model Catalog Downloads**: Cannot browse or download new models from online sources without an internet connection

## Privacy benefits of offline mode

- **Complete Privacy**: Your conversations never leave your local network. No data is sent to cloud servers or third parties
- **Full Control**: You control all your data. Models run on hardware you own or control
- **No Usage Tracking**: Your chats are not monitored, logged, or analyzed by external services
- **Sensitive Information Safe**: Use LMSA offline for confidential documents, proprietary data, or personal information without privacy concerns

## Enabling offline mode

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your local server">
    Launch LM Studio on your computer and load a model, or start an Ollama server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect in LMSA">
    Open LMSA **Settings** and configure your server's local IP address and port.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect from internet (optional)">
    LMSA works whether internet is available or not, but for true offline operation, you may want to disable network connectivity on your mobile device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chat locally">
    Select your local model and start chatting with zero data leaving your network.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Offline vs. online comparison

| Feature | Offline (Local) | Online (Cloud) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internet Required | No | Yes |
| Data Privacy | Maximum | Depends on provider |
| Model Variety | Limited to your server | Hundreds available |
| Web Search | Not available | Available |
| Speed | Fast (local) | Network dependent |
