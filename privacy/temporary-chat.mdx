Temporary Chat lets you have conversations that are **never saved to your chat history**. When enabled, new chats exist only in memory for the current session and disappear as soon as the app is closed or a new chat is started.

## How to enable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Side Menu">
    Open the **Side Menu** and tap **Temporary Chat** (spy icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or use the quick-action button">
    Alternatively, tap the **Temporary Chat** quick-action button in the message input bar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When active, the icon turns purple and a badge appears in the header.

## Key behaviors

- **Not saved to storage:** Temporary chats are never written to your device's local storage, so they won't appear in your **Saved Chats** list.
- **Session-only:** The conversation lives in memory. Closing the app or starting a new chat permanently discards it. There is no recovery.
- **Persisted toggle:** The Temporary Chat on/off setting itself is remembered across restarts, but not the temporary chat contents.
- **New chat required:** You cannot convert an existing chat into a temporary one. Enabling Temporary Chat mid-conversation will prompt you to start a fresh temporary chat instead.
- **Hidden from history:** Temporary chats are excluded from the sidebar chat list, export, and any search.

<Warning>
  Temporary Chat only controls LMSA's in-app history and local app storage. Depending on your provider, prompts and responses may still be logged outside the app: by your Local Server (LM Studio/Ollama) based on its own logging settings, or by OpenRouter/other cloud API providers according to their policies.
</Warning>
