This guide walks you from installation to your first chat. After you finish, you can explore advanced features like templates, file attachments, and voice mode.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install LMSA">
    Download LMSA from the Google Play Store and open the app. The first time you launch it, the Quick Start Guide appears automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your language">
    Pick the language you want LMSA to display in. You can change this later in **Settings** under **Device & Privacy**.

    Currently supported: English, Spanish, Russian, German, and Simplified Chinese.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a connection">
    On the home screen, tap the provider you want to use:

    - **Local Server**: Connect to [LM Studio](/providers/lm-studio) or [Ollama](/providers/ollama) running on your computer for fully private, offline chat.
    - **OpenRouter**: Access hundreds of cloud models with an API key. See the [OpenRouter setup guide](/providers/openrouter).
    - **Custom API**: Connect to any [OpenAI-compatible endpoint](/providers/custom-endpoint), self-hosted or cloud.

    You can change providers anytime by opening the side menu and tapping the provider name at the bottom.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and apply">
    Enter the required details for your chosen provider (server address, API key, or endpoint URL), then tap **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first chat">
    Type a message and tap send. Chats save automatically, and you can switch models anytime from the sidebar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional next steps

After your first chat, the Quick Start Guide may prompt you with two optional settings:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Enable notifications">
    Allow notification permissions so you can control voice-mode text-to-speech playback from your lock screen and notification shade. You can skip this and enable it later in your device settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Keep generating in background">
    Allow LMSA to keep generating replies when you switch to another app or lock your screen. You will see a progress notification while a response is being written, and a completion alert when it is done. You can change this anytime in **Settings** under **Device & Privacy**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Info>
  If you ever want to revisit the Quick Start Guide, you can access it from the app help section.
</Info>
