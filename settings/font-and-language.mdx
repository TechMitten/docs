You can personalize how text looks in your conversations and choose the language the app displays throughout the interface.

## Change the chat bubble font

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open the sidebar and tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Font step">
    Navigate to the **Font** step in Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose font and size">
    Use **Chat Bubble Font** to choose your preferred font style, and **Chat Bubble Font Size** to choose your preferred text size.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Changing **Chat Bubble Font Size** only affects text inside chat bubbles (your messages and AI replies). It does **not** change text size in menus, settings screens, or other parts of the app UI.
</Info>

## Change the app language

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open the sidebar and tap **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Device and Privacy">
    Navigate to the **Device and Privacy** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a language">
    Under **App Language**, choose your preferred language card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Confirm** to apply translations across the entire app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported languages

Currently supported: **English (US)**, **Spanish**, **Russian**, **German**, and **Simplified Chinese**. Additional languages are actively in development and will be added in upcoming updates.

<Warning>
  Translations from English were generated using machine learning and may contain slight inaccuracies. UI language settings change the interface, menus, and options; your AI model will respond in whichever language you speak or write to it in chat.
</Warning>
