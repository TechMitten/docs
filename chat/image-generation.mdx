LMSA includes a powerful AI image generation feature that allows you to create images directly within your chat.

<Note>
  AI Image Generation is a **Premium** feature. It is not available on the Free tier.
</Note>

## How to use

Type `/image` followed by a detailed description of what you want to create.

Example:

```text
/image a futuristic city at sunset with flying cars
```

## Generation limits

- **Free Tier**: Image generation is not available.
- **Premium Tier**: Unlimited image generation.

## Important information

- **Third-party API**: Image generation depends on external APIs and may not always be available.
- **Performance**: Generations may slow down or fail during periods of high demand.
- **Privacy**: Image prompts are sent to third-party providers and are **not private**. Avoid including sensitive data in image prompts.
