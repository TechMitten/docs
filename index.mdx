Connect to a local server on your home network or tap into cloud models with your own API key. Your conversations stay on your device.

## Get up and running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download LMSA">
    Install LMSA from the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lmsa.app). Android 6.0 or higher is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your AI provider">
    Launch LM Studio or Ollama on your PC, or grab an API key from [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai). See [Connecting AI Providers](/providers/lm-studio) for step-by-step guides.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the connection">
    Open **Settings** in LMSA, select your provider type, and enter your server address or API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Select a model, choose a persona or start fresh, and begin your conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
